About
Julian

Julian (he/him) followed an academic path that led to a PhD in physics with a focus in biophysics and neuroscience, along with over a dozen peer-reviewed publications and the founding of an AI art authentication company. Alongside that work, he has always been drawn to building things that bring people together — tools, spaces, and experiences designed around connection and gathering.

After completing psilocybin facilitator training in Oregon, Julian shifted his focus toward work that puts presence and somatic awareness front and center. His approach is rooted in consent, care, and clear communication — creating well-held spaces where people feel safe enough to show up authentically, move at their own pace, and reconnect with themselves and others.
