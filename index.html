About Julian

Julian (he/they) earned a PhD in physics with a focus in biophysics and neuroscience, publishing more than a dozen peer-reviewed papers before founding an AI art authentication company. Through all of it, he's been drawn to building things that bring people together — tools, spaces, and experiences designed around connection and gathering.

He later trained as a facilitator through Synaptic and completed pathways training with Sacred Garden Community, deepening a longstanding focus on presence, somatic awareness, and consent-based practice. His approach is simple: create well-held spaces where people feel safe enough to show up authentically, move at their own pace, and reconnect with themselves and others.
